Mocha Coffee

Description

Coffee is a globally popular beverage brewed from the roasted and ground seeds of the tropical evergreen Coffea plant.

It is widely consumed for its distinctively complex flavor profile, prominent bitterness, and energizing, stimulating properties derived from its natural caffeine content

Ingredients

  • 1 cup hot brewed coffee
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Pour hot coffee into a mug. Stir in cocoa, sugar, and milk
